About

From Egypt to São Paulo: how a habit of fixing my own problems became my work.

I build the tools I wish existed, then I ship them.

I'm Girgis, a self-taught developer from Egypt, now based in São Paulo. I started the way many developers do: with a problem I wanted gone and nobody to build the fix for me. So I learned to build it myself.

That became a habit. Find a slow, manual job and replace it with something that runs on its own. Since 2021 the habit has grown into web automation, systems that real businesses run on, and Android apps that are live on Google Play.

Moving to Brazil gave me new problems to solve. I needed Portuguese, so I built Lu Lingo and Gesr. I needed an immigration appointment that never seemed to open, so I built a watcher that told my phone the moment one did.

Today I build the same way for clients: start from the real problem, ship something that works, and keep it working.

How I work

  1. 01

    Real problems first

    I start from the job that is slow or broken today, not from a technology I want to try.

  2. 02

    Shipped, then maintained

    My apps get updates and store-policy fixes long after launch. Done means live, and still working.

  3. 03

    Reviewed and tested

    I use modern tools, AI-assisted development included, and I review and test everything myself before it ships.

  4. 04

    One person, the whole way

    Design, build, publishing and support, handled by one person who knows every part of the project.
